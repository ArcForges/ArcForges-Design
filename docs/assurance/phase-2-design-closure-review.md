## 1. Authority and completion meaning

The amended requirements and recorded decisions govern this repair. The existing implementation repository remains the reconciliation target, at the recorded `ede43db` baseline; scaffolded projects are not evidence of product behaviour. Reference provenance and its permitted reading/reuse boundaries remain those of the [reference matrices](reference-coverage/README.md). Immutable inputs were preserved.

A repair closes here only when its required behaviour, state ownership, failure/recovery mechanism, implementation placement and meaningful future evidence are explicit. A future gate is legitimate when the mechanism is designed and the remaining question requires running the real implementation. An undefined schema, impossible dependency or ambiguous rule citation cannot be left as such a gate.

The [workflow traces](end-to-end-workflow-verification.md) were rewritten against the resulting mechanisms. The [verification artifact](design-repair-verification.md) contains the executable counterexamples and complete corpus checker. This review does not authorise a new product scope or reopen the accepted commercial model.
