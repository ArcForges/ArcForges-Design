# Deprecated Design Inputs

**Status: DEPRECATED — historical archive, not carried into this repository.** The four original inputs completed their role in producing ArcForges' formal requirements, decisions, architecture and implementation plans. Under [P2-019](../decisions/phase-2-specification-decisions.md#rule-p2-019) their bodies are not part of this repository. They remain archived byte-for-byte at `docs/deprecated-inputs/` in the derivation-baseline commit `06f3513dd86ffdab6f417d5fa4f2bce99037c338` that P2-019 records, and in this repository's history before the derivation.

Current work follows the effective accepted [decisions](../decisions/README.md), [requirements](../requirements/README.md), [architecture](../architecture/README.md), [planning](../planning/README.md) and [assurance](../assurance/README.md). Do not extract new commitments from the archive, reconcile its internal contradictions, or use it to restore excluded product scope. If a current design rule is missing or incomplete, resolve that gap in the formal design.

The archive may contain obsolete names, superseded decisions, contradictions and unvalidated or outdated claims. Their presence is historical, not a current design conflict or a new audit obligation. Historical citations and completed input-coverage counts do not put these files back into the active reading or audit denominator.
