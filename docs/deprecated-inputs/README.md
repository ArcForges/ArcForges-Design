# Deprecated Design Inputs

**Status: DEPRECATED — historical archive only.** These original inputs have completed their role in producing ArcForges' formal requirements, decisions, architecture and implementation plans. They are excluded from ongoing requirements discovery, design-completeness audits and implementation planning.

Current work follows the effective accepted [decisions](../decisions/README.md), [requirements](../requirements/README.md), [architecture](../architecture/README.md), [planning](../planning/README.md) and [assurance](../assurance/README.md). Do not extract new commitments from this archive, reconcile its internal contradictions, or use it to restore excluded product scope. If a current design rule is missing or incomplete, resolve that gap in the formal design.

The archive may contain obsolete names, superseded decisions, contradictions and unvalidated or outdated claims. Their presence is historical, not a current design conflict or a new audit obligation. Historical citations and completed input-coverage counts do not put these files back into the active reading or audit denominator. Audits of the current design and reference-source repositories retain their own scope.
