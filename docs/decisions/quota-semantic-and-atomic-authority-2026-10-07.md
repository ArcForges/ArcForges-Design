## Exact production contract obligations

The primitive semantic validator captures canonical bytes defensively and returns an immutable owner descriptor set or typed invalid/unavailable/conflict result. Queries bind realm/workspace, current positive Entitlement revision/snapshot version, definitions version/profile hash, exact immutable Config revision/document hash and actual selected term. No API accepts `approved`, `hasPermission`, `newPeriod` or a caller-supplied unit as authority. Public authenticated requests retain actual session/operation-owner authorization; internal context identifiers alone grant no permission.

The server-only Config source exposes current and exact historical approved associations. A current association includes realm, active revision/document hash and exact signed owner-artifact ID/profile/hash, fully verified byte length and owner definitions version. The Config participant seals the current head and association for one exact family/plan/owner scope. The Workspace participant resolves real owned state/permission and contributes its actual current guard. Entitlement prepares its basis and kernel contribution for that same immutable command. Every contribution is checked under its actual issuer, family, plan and scope. A forged, missing, foreign or stale contribution executes nothing. The coordinator uses one fixed batch and one receipt/outbox/archive tail; no HTTP, R2 or directory call runs inside it.

All ports require cancellation, defensive copies and bounded work. Ordinary missing source differs from a stale/denied principal and an unknown mutation outcome; the caller cannot reinterpret any of them as zero usage or authorization. Context and measurement fields are canonical command data whose authority is checked by real owners. Receipt retention and owner archive lifecycle follow the actual existing command receipt protocol; no shorter semantic-profile TTL may invalidate an accepted outstanding reservation. Referenced immutable profiles and measurement/basis receipts remain retained while an outstanding reservation, object copy or accepted replay/reconciliation can require them; deletion needs an explicit audited retention decision after all such references are gone.

The closed family registrations are `quota-accounting` for `publish-budget` and `reserve`, and `resource-quota-accounting` for `admit-upload`, `promote-object` and `release-verified-copy`. Exact role arrays, owners and SQL are generated from the implementation's real bounded parameters, not a permissive catch-all registry. Config and Workspace are read-only participants for new publication/admission; Entitlement and Resource remain the write owners of their own tables. Settlement never requires a new paid term, but still requires current operation/recovery/lease permission and the pinned original basis/measurement. Compiler admission is limited to those closed quota roles and Entitlement-owned validity predicates, preserving Identity-only CLOUD.78 expiry/due metadata and all existing hash-bound families. Bound publication is one budget; reservation retains the existing at-most-eight distinct budget items. No speculative unbounded workload is admitted.

New budget publication and reservation bind the actual current profile and term and append their immutable basis. Gauge publication never resets used/held, including downgrades. EntitlementPeriod identity comes from the normalized immutable paid interval, not the subscription, calendar or an evaluation counter. Highest-priority/stable-ID selection, delayed authorization, applicable actions and grace exclusion follow TM-01 through TM-05. Switching away and back reuses the original budget. Current term expiration rejects new work at the SQL authorization guard; accepted old-period work settles against its pinned basis. Replayed original commands retain their original receipts and effects and cannot create a new budget under current policy.

The three new Entitlement tables and Resource measurement receipt are explicit expand migrations. The integration owner allocates numbers at the actual accepted baseline; existing migration/profile/history bytes are immutable. Profile and basis readers are bounded/paginated and fail closed on corrupt bytes, negative or missing revisions, mismatched hashes, unknown units or unavailable authority. The new private profile has at most 64 definitions and 65536 canonical UTF8 bytes; this does not narrow published ConfigurationDocument, operator context, public reply or transport bounds.
