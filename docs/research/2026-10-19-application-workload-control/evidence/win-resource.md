# Windows job objects and resource control of applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's Windows owner-directed resource-control track (report 11), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-win-resource-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned, so a fact that is not published leaves a gap. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n"; the digest and report are working files and are not published).
- **Not published:** D2-win-resource-35 … -38. They are observations about delivered or planned release-1 texts, code and verification entries; they were reported to the owning tasks' reviewers. For the same reason, the clauses of D2-win-resource-9 and -12 that commented on delivered release-1 wording and classification are removed.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process (attach); L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - (A) means the process-level dimensions (files, network, registry, processes, resources, storage); (B) means the GUI interaction channels (input, screen, window messages, clipboard), which this study does not isolate.
  - P0 is a standard user; P1 a one-time administrator step. Strength words and the adversary classes A1 … A6 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s, with HINT as proposed in chapter 17 §3.2.
  - Default target: a process of the same user and session, at medium integrity, unprotected. ENFORCED holds against A1 and A2; against code that targets ArcForges' named job it is a GUARDRAIL (verification entry V-W11).
  - "E4 (inferred from E2 text)" means reasoned from vendor text; it stays E4 until a lab confirms it (the track wrote "E4 (inferred from E2 text)", D2-gf02-vocabulary-13).
  - Status words follow the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12); words the track wrote before that reconciliation are mapped in place (see "Review corrections").
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts and `LAB-…` entries are the 2026-10-18 record's. `V-W…` IDs are entries of the [verification register](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/verification/platform-capabilities.md).
