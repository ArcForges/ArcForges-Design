# Access-denial diagnosis for confined applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on access-denial diagnosis and compatibility reporting (report 33), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** nothing is implemented or lab-tested; rules, fields and statuses are E4 proposals unless labelled. Design points are proposals, not changes.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host: facts the study read on one build are stated as per-build checks.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf05-access-denial-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 33).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe … L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is a standard user; P1 a one-time owner group or policy step; P2 elevation per use.
  - Diagnoses hold against A1 and A2 only (Design 11's T-22; chapter 17's T-39). "Denial" means an (A) access refused by an OS mechanism or by an ArcForges mediator; no (B) isolation is assumed.
  - Denial shapes: **S1** a refusal (`EACCES`, `EPERM`, `STATUS_ACCESS_DENIED`); **S2** an absence (`ENOENT` in a view, no route in N0, `ServiceUnknown`, an AppContainer loopback timeout); **S3** a divergence (redirected writes, remapped objects).
  - The diagnosis schema is the study's one schema (D2-gf02-vocabulary-14 … -21); the access rules are `DX-ACC-00` … `-13` (D2-gf05-access-denial-33). "Rank n" is the report's ranking of its proposals.
  - [Chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) is the Design text recorded on 2026-10-18. `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts, `LAB-…` entries and the rulings C-20, C-35 and C-37 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
