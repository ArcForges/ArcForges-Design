# Third-party workload classes: media, IDEs and services: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on third-party workload classes (report 34: media production and rendering, IDEs and background indexing, long-running services and utilities), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; nothing was measured. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf06-workload-classes-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section or finding of report 34).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is a standard user.
  - Evidence: E2 is reserved to OS vendors; application vendors' pages (Microsoft's Visual Studio, VS Code and OneDrive pages included) are E3; pointers are at most E5; analysis at most E4; SPECIFIED is Design text with a planned task, not E1.
  - Status words are the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12): engine limits and application knobs are `tool_enforced`, OBSERVED; a priority an application sets itself is `self_applied`; Flatpak-delegated (T-FP) is a DELEGATED lifecycle with `policy_source = PACKAGE`, OBSERVED. ENFORCED holds against A1 and A2 only.
  - Placement: owner placement sits beside the root, inside `U`, never touched by the ladder or the guard; managed placement sits inside `B`; the role is HUMAN-only (D2-gf01-placement-1, -6).
  - The model: no new concept. The placement reconciliation's two classes (interactive, `OWNER` by default; background service, `MANAGED` by default), `COMMAND` tasks and five traits suffice: `realtime_threads` (the CPU number becomes a weight; real-time rlimits are read back), `own_supervisor` (`OOMPolicy=continue`; restart owner APPLICATION), `persistent_helpers` (`ROOT_PROCESS`; daemon switches), `uses_gpu` (deferred while the owner is present) and `locked_memory` (a hard limit below the locked set is refused). Named classes stay catalogue values only; a catalogue match only suggests, and an `OWNER` class binds only after HUMAN confirmation.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `V-…` IDs are verification-register entries.
