# Linux process-level confinement tiers per display system: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's Linux GUI-application confinement track (report 14), including its adversarial verification log and the verifier's additions, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections". Every ArcForges value is pre-lab, at most E4 ("can enforce (probed)").
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-lnx-confine-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n", and of report 14 where the digest names one).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - Tiers: **T-WL** Wayland plus bwrap plus a filtered bus on a `wp_security_context_v1` compositor; **T-WP** plain Wayland plus bwrap with no bus and no X on Mutter 51 or Weston 15; **T-X** X11 plus bwrap; **T-LL** Landlock only; **T-FP** delegated to Flatpak. N0 and N1 are chapter 17's network-namespace modes.
  - Channel classes: an **(A)-escape** starts or steers work outside the confinement (a launcher or drive cell); an **(A)-leak** exposes protected data only; a **(B)-only** channel concerns GUI interaction alone and is never counted against (A). GUI display, window and input isolation is outside the study's scope; (B) channels appear here only where they decide a process-level guarantee.
  - [Chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) §3.2's rules: an escape-class gap caps every dimension at {A1, A2}; an unmeasured launcher cell counts as one; A4 is unattainable today.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts, `LAB-…` entries and the rulings C-35, M-7 and M-8 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
