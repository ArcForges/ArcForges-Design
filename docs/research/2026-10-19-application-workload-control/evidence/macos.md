# macOS capability statements: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's macOS boundaries track (report 15), including its two verification logs, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Capability statements only.** macOS stays a planned future execution target. Nothing here makes an Apple hardware, developer-program, lab, architecture-coverage or detailed implementation decision; those belong to macOS development. No macOS fact is E1: nothing was run. "E3 (source)" means Apple's published source code.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-macos-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the macOS releases. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n").
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. The macOS **launch paths** are written LP1 (Launch Services), LP2 (direct spawn) and LP3 (a per-application launchd job); they are not integration levels.
  - (A) means the process-level dimensions; (B) the GUI interaction channels, which this study does not isolate. {A1, A2} (accidental and over-privileged code) is the most an escape-class realization holds against (chapter 17).
  - "Third-party" means closed-source and not App-Sandboxed. Controls reach same-user processes only. "All" means every supported macOS release (.NET 10 supports 15, 26 and 27; 27 runs on Apple silicon only).
  - Status words follow the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12); the words the track proposed (`PRIORITY_ONLY`, `MONITORED`, `ADVISORY`, `VENDOR_SIGNATURE`) are mapped in place (see "Review corrections").
  - `LAB2-MAC-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md); their venues are chosen when macOS development starts. `D-macos-…` facts, `LAB-…` entries and the ruling C-42 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
