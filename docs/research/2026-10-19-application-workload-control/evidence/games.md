# Games and interactive workloads: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's games and interactive-workloads track (report 17), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; nothing is a measured ArcForges result. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-games-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n", and of report 17 where the digest names one).
- **Not published:** D2-games-9, an observation about the planned release-1 sampler, reported to the owning task's reviewer.
- **Bottom line of the track:** the value comes from coordinating the managed background work ArcForges already controls, with no second scheduler, and with the game untouched by default. Stages: (a) the owner toggle, a reserve profile, the Linux slice weight and the freeze of pausable work; (b) detection and the user-away inverse; (c) Windows QoS and priority; (d) cooperative channels; (e) GPU deferral. A stage is rejected if its measured gain (LAB2-X-06) is within noise on both machine classes while throughput drops past the owner's tolerance, if detection exceeds a stated false-positive bound, or if LAB2-X-08 shows an action breaks agent sessions. The baseline to beat is pausing the `agents` pool. Anti-cheat titles are never tested (an exclusion by rule).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is a standard user.
  - SPECIFIED means Design-specified and planned for release 1, not demonstrated; AF-RES-01, AF-SCHED-01 and AF-WSL-01 are planned. E1 in delivered code covers only attempt weights, the startup root `CpuRate`, the Linux freeze and the process snapshot.
  - The touch ladder on the game: T0 nothing; T1 handle-free reads; T2 a query-only handle; T3 changing its environment without opening it; T4 its scheduling; T5 ownership; T6 its execution or memory.
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [11](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/11-security-privacy.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)); T-22 and T-23 are Design 11's threats. Status words are the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12).
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
