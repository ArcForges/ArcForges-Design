# The cost model and maintenance burden: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on the cost model for performance and maintenance (report 39), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; nothing ran. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** research only. ArcForges costs are at most E4. **est.** means arithmetic or judgement with its assumption stated, never a measurement; **SPECIFIED** means designed in the Design, not demonstrated; **pointer** means one post or community list, below E3. No macOS decision; no release-1 change.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf11-cost-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels ("lab" means measured on a lab venue, not a product feature), whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section, maintenance item M-n or decision P-n of report 39).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - The interactive cost measures ICM-1 … ICM-8, the ceilings Y-start, Y-input, Y-frame, Y-hang, Y-mem-out and Y-mem-in, the maintenance items M-1 … M-12 and the decisions P-1 … P-8 are defined by the facts below. Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [15](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/15-performance-targets.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)).
  - "E4 (inferred from E2 text)" is the track's "E2-derived" (D2-gf02-vocabulary-13).
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `LAB-…` entries and gates G01 … G41 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
