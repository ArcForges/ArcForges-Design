# Storage budgets, capacity constructions and private data: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's storage-budgets, application-private-data and data-exchange track (report 18), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; values are pre-lab, not realized ArcForges guarantees. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-storage-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n"). "Analysis" is the report's own reasoning, at most E4.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is a standard user; P1 a one-time administrator or root step.
  - Adversary classes as in chapter 17; {A1, A2} is chapter 17's cap when a realization has an escape-class gap.
  - Storage kinds (the track's names): **CAPACITY** (the file system refuses the write); **ADMISSION** (ArcForges refuses the next launch); **MONITORED** (ArcForges measures, then acts; overshoot = write rate × (interval + stop latency)); **COOPERATIVE** (the application evicts); UNAVAILABLE(reason). Scope: **DECLARED** (declared roots) or **COMPLETE** (all grants of an enforced `fs.write`, minus gaps). The study's one vocabulary writes them as the mechanisms `fs_capacity`, `admission_threshold` (GUARDRAIL), `monitored_limit` and `tool_enforced` (OBSERVED), and the scope COMPLETE as `ALL_WRITES` (D2-gf02-vocabulary-2, -11, -12).
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [06](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/06-storage-workspaces.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)); R-… IDs are the Design's requirements.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts, `LAB-…` entries and the ruling C-42 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `L-…` IDs are verification-register entries.
