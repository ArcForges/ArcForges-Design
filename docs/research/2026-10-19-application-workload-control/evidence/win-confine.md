# Windows process-level confinement of GUI and headless applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's Windows GUI-application confinement track (report 12), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections". Every value is attainable and pre-lab, not a realized ArcForges guarantee.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-win-confine-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned, so a fact that is not published leaves a gap. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n").
- **Not published:** D2-win-confine-32, a separate-desktop remedy for interactive applications. GUI display, window and input isolation is outside the study's scope (the scope correction of 2026-10-19); the facts that decide a process-level guarantee (the drive and launcher cells, the disclosed clipboard) are kept.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - Windows tiers: W0 job only (release 1); W1 write-restricted token, Low integrity and an alternate desktop; W2 a regular AppContainer (AC); W3 a less-privileged AppContainer (LPAC). IL is integrity level; AAP is the ALL APPLICATION PACKAGES group.
  - (A) means the process-level dimensions; (B) the GUI interaction channels, which this study does not isolate. A1 … A5 and `holds_against` are as in [chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) §3.2 and §4.1.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts and `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s. "(pointer)" marks a source below E3 that only points to a question.
