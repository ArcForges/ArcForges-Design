# Limit-failure diagnosis and application identity: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's failure-diagnostics and per-application identity track (report 19), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** nothing here is implemented; the verdict taxonomy, the identity anchors and the identity rules are E4 proposals, none lab-tested. This track confines nothing: "enforceable" means checks the node makes itself.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host: facts the study read on one build are stated as per-build checks.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-diagnostics-identity-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n", and of report 19 where the digest names one).
- **Not published:** D2-diagnostics-identity-19, a list of observations about delivered release-1 records, reported to the owning tasks' reviewers. For the same reason, the clauses of D2-diagnostics-identity-3, -15, -16 and -17 that commented on delivered release-1 behaviour are removed.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - Diagnoses and unconfined identities hold against A1 and A2 only: same-user code can change containers (Design 11, T-22) and policy (chapter 17, T-39).
  - Three identity questions: **Q1** which profile applies to a launch; **Q2** which running processes belong to an application; **Q3** which application calls the cooperative API. None is chapter 17 §7.2's attempt identity.
  - The verdicts and causes are the study's one diagnosis schema (D2-gf02-vocabulary-14 … -21). This track's rules L1–L14, W1–W13 and M1–M5 were renamed `DX-LNX-nn`, `DX-WIN-nn` and `DX-MAC-nn`, and the reconciled rule sets of D2-gf02-vocabulary-22 … -26 govern.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
