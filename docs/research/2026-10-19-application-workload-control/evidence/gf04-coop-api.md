# The cooperative API surface and the caller identity ladder: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's reconciliation of the cooperative API surface (report 32), which merged the endpoint, declaration, reservation and identity proposals of the track reports, including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** nothing of the surface exists in code. Every proposal is at most E4, on a SPECIFIED or planned scheduler (AF-SCHED-01, AF-RES-01). All names are illustrative, not reserved (chapter 17 §12).
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf04-coop-api-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 32).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [11](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/11-security-privacy.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)); R1 … R7 are chapter 17's inheritance rules; strength words are chapter 17's, and the status vocabulary is D2-gf02-vocabulary's. "LocalRpc" is DesktopPlatform's local RPC transport ([README](https://github.com/ArcForges/DesktopPlatform/blob/main/src/BuildingBlocks/ArcForges.LocalRpc/README.md)).
  - The declaration levels are named **undesignated**, **designated** and **owner toggle**; the hold term is written `K` per resource (Design 03 §10 as amended).
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `V-W…` and `V-L…` are verification-register entries.
