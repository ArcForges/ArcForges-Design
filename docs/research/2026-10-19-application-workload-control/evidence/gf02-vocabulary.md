# The status vocabulary and the diagnosis schema: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's reconciliation of the status vocabulary and the failure-diagnosis schema (report 31), which merged the tracks' status words and diagnosis rules into one vocabulary, including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** every vocabulary item and `DX-*` rule is **E4 as a design** (no lab has run), resting on E1–E3 facts the track reports verified; a composite takes its weakest part. Release 1 is untouched: release-1 values are never renamed, and every proposal is additive, at a row's promotion.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf02-vocabulary-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 31).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is a standard user; P1 one administrator step.
  - [Chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) is the Design text recorded on 2026-10-18. Every diagnosis holds against {A1, A2} only, not against code that alters ArcForges' containers.
  - The rule IDs `DX-ALL-nn`, `DX-LNX-nn`, `DX-WIN-nn`, `DX-MAC-nn`, `DX-STO-nn` and `DX-GRT-nn` are defined in D2-gf02-vocabulary-22 … -26 (and `DX-ACC-nn` in D2-gf05-access-denial-33); the Design cites these facts, not the rule IDs.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `V-L…` IDs are verification-register entries.
