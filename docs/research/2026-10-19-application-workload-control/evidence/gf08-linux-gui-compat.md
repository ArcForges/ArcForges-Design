# Linux compatibility surfaces of confined GUI applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on Linux GUI compatibility surfaces (report 36: packaging formats, settings stores, input methods and accessibility, LSM per-application profiles), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections". Every ArcForges value is pre-lab, at most E4.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf08-linux-gui-compat-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 36).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox.
  - Tiers as in [lnx-confine.md](lnx-confine.md): T-WL (Wayland, bwrap, a filtered bus, a security-context compositor), T-WP (plain Wayland, no bus), T-X (X11 plus bwrap), T-LL (Landlock only), T-FP (delegated to Flatpak); "views" are T-WL, T-WP and T-X. NNP is `no_new_privs`.
  - [Chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) §3.2: an escape-class gap, or an unmeasured launcher cell, caps every dimension at {A1, A2}.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts and `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
