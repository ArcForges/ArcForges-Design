# Language-neutral cooperative clients: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on language-neutral cooperative clients (report 38), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** nothing is built; ArcForges compositions are at most E4, never guarantees; release 1 is untouched. Every client path is L5 and needs cooperation; owner-directed enforcement never depends on it. E1-DP means delivered in DesktopPlatform's LocalRpc, not yet in the node; SPECIFIED means Design text only. Interoperation of non-.NET clients with LocalRpc is E4 until LAB2-X-05.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf10-clients-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 38).
- **Reading notes:**
  - "The node endpoint" is the existing user-wide endpoint; in stage 1 the cooperative service is served there, with an application client kind (D2-gf04-coop-api-2). A cooperative-only listener exists only as a deferred stage, for callers in sandboxes ArcForges did not create (D2-gf04-coop-api-5).
  - The protocol obligations are labelled W-1 … W-4 (Windows), L-1 … L-3 (Linux) and B-1 … B-4 (both); MUST and SHOULD are proposed protocol text.
  - Design 11's threat T-22 is same-user tampering with ArcForges' objects, which keeps same-user values at GUARDRAIL.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
