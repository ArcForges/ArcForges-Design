# Owner-directed controls against an application working against the owner: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on tamper resistance (report 35), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; every A3 value is attainable and pre-lab ("can enforce (probed)"). Like the report, this file describes no technique.
- **Process-level conclusions only.** As the owner's scope correction of 2026-10-19 directs, this file holds only the gap-fill's process-level conclusions: the strength of owner-directed controls against an unconfined or a confined application; the same-user paths to them; the hygiene measures for ArcForges' own objects; and the requirement field `min_holds_against`. The gap-fill's material on approval-screen integrity, window overlays, presentation and separate signing devices is outside the study's scope and is not published.
- **Not published:** D2-gf07-tamper-human-4, -6, -23 … -29 and -32 … -39 (display protection, approval provenance and attestation, the integrity and presentation of human approval surfaces, separate-device approval and secure desktops; and, within them, observations about planned release-1 Desktop and origin tasks, which were reported to the owning tasks' reviewers). D2-gf07-tamper-human-40 is published without its approval-surface items.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf07-tamper-human-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 35).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 an OS-enforced sandbox; L5 SDK. Adversary classes (chapter 17 §1.3): A1 accidental; A2 over-reaching but honest; A3 hostile within its channels; A5 defeats the mechanism; A6 privileged. **Unconfined** means L1–L3, running as the user; **confined** means L4 (W2/W3, AppContainer or LPAC; the Linux view chain or the Landlock-only tier).
  - The measure labels TM-01 … TM-08 are defined by the facts below. A **HUMAN-origin client** is one of ArcForges' own interfaces that acts with the owner's authority (the Desktop, an interactive CLI); the display path into it is a drive cell, counted with the launcher cells.
  - Design threats T-05, T-22, T-23 and T-26 are Design 11's; T-39 is [chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)'s; B0 is Design 01's boundary between the node and its own workload objects.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `L-…`, `V-W…` and `V-L…` IDs are verification-register entries.
