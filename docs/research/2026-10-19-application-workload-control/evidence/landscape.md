# The landscape of comparable tools and the rejected product directions: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's landscape, prior-art and opportunities track (report 1b), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections". A product's own documentation is E3 for "the tool does X", never E2; marketing pages and forum posts were used only as demand pointers and are not cited here.
- **Bottom line:** kernel quotas on launched or adopted applications exist today, and per-application quotas for unmodified programs are not new (Process Governor). What no product the study found offers is the combination: kernel quotas, access restriction, lifecycle and failure evidence for one application under one owner policy, reported requested versus enforced per limit. Hints are never limits, and a (B) channel through which confined code drives unconfined code caps a claim at {A1, A2}.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Not published:** D2-landscape-27, three observations about delivered release-1 code and texts, reported to the owning tasks' reviewers. The report's ranked opportunity list, its open questions and its lab proposals are not facts; the lab proposals are entries of the [lab register](../lab-register.md), and the rejected directions are recorded as AD-0017 boundaries 3.11 … 3.13 as amended.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-landscape-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned, so a fact that is not published leaves a gap. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 1b).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 … P3 are the privilege levels of [chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md) §1.3 (P0 a standard user, P1 one administrator step, P3 a kernel component).
  - (A) means the process-level dimensions (files, private data, network, registry, process tree, CPU, memory, storage); (B) the GUI interaction channels, which this study does not isolate. A1 … A6, the strength words and `holds_against` are as in chapter 17 §1.3 and §3.2. HINT is defined in this folder's README.
  - Windows tiers: W0 job only (release 1); W2 a regular AppContainer; W3 a less-privileged AppContainer. "est." marks the report's unmeasured estimate, below E4.
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [11](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/11-security-privacy.md), 17); T-22 and T-23 are Design 11's threats; AD-0017 boundaries are numbered as in [AD-0017](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/decisions/AD-0017-delegated-execution-direction.md) §3.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts and `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `V-…` IDs are verification-register entries.
