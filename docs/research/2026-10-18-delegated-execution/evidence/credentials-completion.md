# Evidence record: group budgets and credential mediation (credentials-completion)

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study reports 17 (group budgets and credential isolation) and 17b (credential mediation, completion), including their verification logs (their `[Verified …]` markers override the original text), as condensed in the study's credentials-completion decision digest (§1).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report (17 or 17b) the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Use:** input to roadmap direction. Strength words (ENFORCED, PARTIAL, GUARDRAIL, OBSERVED, NONE, MEDIATED), adversaries A1–A6 and privileges P0–P3 follow the study vocabulary. "AF Pn / WL Pn" means the privilege ArcForges needs / the privilege the workload needs. Every ArcForges-integration claim stays E4 until the matching lab plan of the study passes.
