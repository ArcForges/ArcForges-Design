# Evidence record: Linux kernel and systemd mechanisms (track lnx-kernel)

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts. It informs roadmap direction only and is not release-1 verification.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Source reports:** the study report 11a (Linux kernel and systemd), including its inline verification and reconciliation markers and its verification log, which override the original text, and the study's lnx-kernel track digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet).
- **Conventions:** privileges are written "AF Pn / WL Pn" (ArcForges / workload). "Hostile" means a same-user workload that targets ArcForges' own objects. Estimates are marked "est.". Report sections refer to the study report 11a; "verif. #n" refers to its verification log.
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 11a the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.

## Notes on evidence corrections carried into this record

- **ptrace (verif. #34, refuted):** the user namespace is not a ptrace barrier; row 21 carries the corrected position.
- **cgroup tampering (verif. #23–25; study reconciliation C9):** a same-user workload can leave its unit cgroup only into a leaf of the delegated subtree, and raised limits stay bounded by the root-owned delegated root; rows 1 and 17 use this corrected scope.
- **BPF unit options (verif. #20):** ignored with a one-time warning rather than fully silently; the decision to avoid them (row 8) is unchanged.
- **Landlock ABI mapping (verif. #2, #6–8):** ABI 8 = Linux 7.0, ABI 9 = 7.1, ABI 10 = 7.2, ABI 11 unreleased; rows 10 and 11 use these values.
- **Fedora 44 kernel (verif. #35):** reported on 7.1.x (ABI 9) from secondary sources only; Fedora is outside the AD-0002 supported list.
- **Debian user-namespace default (verif. #38):** unverifiable from the cited source; Debian behaviour stays E4.
