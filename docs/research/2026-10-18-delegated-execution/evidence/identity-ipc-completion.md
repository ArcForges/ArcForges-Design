# Caller identity, IPC reach and cache trust: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 16 (identity, IPC and service reachability: items 1–9 and its 47-row verification log; the report is truncated in item 10) and the study report 16b (identity and IPC, completion: items 10–11, deliverables D1–D4, the unexpected connections, the combined ending and its 40-row independent verification log), with the fact sheet in the study's identity-ipc-completion digest (section 1).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report (16 or 16b) the fact was drawn from. The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Reading notes:** Where a verification marker corrected the original text, the corrected statement and label are used. E1-DP marks first-party DesktopPlatform code that the node consumes. Strength words (ENFORCED, PARTIAL, GUARDRAIL, OBSERVED) and adversary classes follow the study's working vocabulary; MEDIATED is a proposed strength (study reconciliation C13). Tier names (W0, W-RT, W-LOW, W-AC, W-LPAC, U0; L0–L5, N0–N2; M0, M1) are the study's. "VM plan" refers to the experiment plans in report 16b section 8.4; none has run. No experiment was performed for this record. LAB-… IDs name entries of `lab-register.md` in this folder.

| D-identity-ipc-completion-3 | C-21 | Under W2/W3 the endpoint-bound identity of the attempt's own pipe stands, but the `ipc` dimension as a whole is PARTIAL (gaps `system_brokers` and `com_per_server_permissions`), because out-of-process COM reach depends on each server's permissions; the per-attempt pipe alone does not make `ipc` ENFORCED. |
| D-identity-ipc-completion-4 | C-20 | A leased AppContainer SID is re-leased only after the journal shows every ACE naming it removed, its profile storage and registry location reset, and no process with that token alive; after a node crash it is quarantined, not re-used.
