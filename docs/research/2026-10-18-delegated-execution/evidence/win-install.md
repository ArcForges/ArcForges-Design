# Evidence record: Windows installation lifecycle

Scope: research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.

Date: 2026-10-18.

Source reports: the study report 10b (Windows installation lifecycle), including its inline verification and reconciliation markers and its 51-row verification log, and the study's win-install decision digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet). Where a verification marker corrected the original report text, the corrected statement is used here.

Publication notes:
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 10b the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file incomplete. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.

Reading notes:
- "AF" means ArcForges. P0 means no privilege beyond a normal user, P1 a one-time administrator action, P2 a per-use administrator approval (UAC), P3 a kernel driver or production signing certificate.
- Lifecycle levels: OWNED (AF wrote and journaled the state), DELEGATED (the OS or a vendor tool owns it), OBSERVATION (detection or prediction only). Strength terms follow the study: ENFORCED, PARTIAL, OBSERVED, NONE.
- "SPECIFIED" means the ArcForges Design specifies the mechanism but it is not delivered. "(est.)" marks an author estimate, at most E5.
- "The study's VM plans" (10b VM plans 1 to 7) are documentation-only experiment plans; none was run. LAB-… IDs name entries of `lab-register.md` in this folder.
- Section references (§) are to the study report 10b.

## Review corrections

The corrected statement governs where it differs from the claim above. Rulings named C-nn are from the study's consolidated review and M-n from its re-review (2026-10-18); neither is published, so each corrected statement is given in full.

| Fact | Ruling | Corrected statement |
|---|---|---|
| D-win-install-18, D-win-install-19 | C-32; re-review M-1 | On an administrator account without Administrator protection, UAC is not a security boundary; unconfined workloads may obtain administrator rights silently, so "the person approves the prompt" cannot be assumed. Writes of an elevated run land in another account's profile and are reported as `Not known`. The account type and the Administrator protection state are local host facts for a HUMAN-only doctor surface, never for an agent-readable statement. |
