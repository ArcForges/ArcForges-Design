# Evidence record: agent, tool and MCP-server sandboxes; WASI (x-agents)

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts. It informs roadmap direction only and is not release-1 verification.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 13a (agent sandboxes, MCP and WASM), with its inline verification markers and its verification log of 66 checked claims, and its x-agents decision digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet). Corrected text from the verification log and the study reconciliation overrides the original report text.
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). No row describes a particular host.
- **Review corrections:** no ruling of the study's review round of 2026-10-18 changed a value in this file; see the closing section.
- **Strengths:** as documented by each mechanism's own source. ArcForges has probed none of them, so every strength is E4 as an ArcForges value until the corresponding study VM plan runs. LAB-… IDs name entries of `lab-register.md` in this folder.
- **Report section column:** A1–A10, Part B and C1–C2 are sections of the study report 13a; "verification log #n" is row n of that report's verification log; "study reconciliation Cn" is item n of the study's contradiction reconciliation. The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.

## Review corrections

No ruling of the study's review round (2026-10-18) changed a value in this file. The review drew one consequence from D-x-agents-14, -15 and -18 that the facts themselves do not state: a command routed through ArcForges, by an MCP tool or a hook rewrite, runs outside the AI client's own OS sandbox, and ArcForges cannot see that sandbox today. That is a statement about ArcForges for the Design's threat text, not a correction of these rows.
