# Windows security primitives below the account boundary: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study reports 10a (Windows security primitives) and 10d (Windows tokens and the AppContainer ladder), with their inline verification markers and verification logs, as condensed in the study's win-primitives-completion digest (section 1).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated or incomplete. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges, ArcForges/DesktopPlatform and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Labels:** E1 demonstrated in ArcForges; E1-DP first-party DesktopPlatform code outside the node; E2 documented by the OS vendor; E3 demonstrated by a mature third-party project; E4 feasible, needs a prototype; E5 speculative; E6 impractical or inappropriate. P0 means no elevation; P1 means a one-time or recurring administrator step.
- **Tiers:** W0 is today's resource-only job; W1 stacks mitigations, Low integrity, a write-restricted token and a separate desktop; W2 is a hand-built regular AppContainer; W3 is a less-privileged AppContainer (LPAC); U0 is the OS process-sandbox engines.
