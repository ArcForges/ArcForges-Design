# Linux launch chain and managed installation: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Sources:** the study report 11c (Linux launch chain and installation), including its inline `[Verified …]` markers and its 40-row verification log, which override the original text, and the study's linux-chain-install decision digest (section 1, the fact sheet).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions, including installed package revisions, were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 11c the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Use:** roadmap direction only. The integrated chain is E4 as a whole: a design built from E1 parts (the attempt unit, `exec-launch`, the records) and E2/E3 parts (kernel semantics, bubblewrap). No row is a guarantee until the study's launch-chain lab group runs (study reconciliation C8).

Privileges are written "AF Pn / WL Pn" (ArcForges / workload). "Report section" refers to sections of the study report 11c.

## Review corrections

The corrected statement governs where it differs from the claim above. Rulings named C-nn and N-nn are from the study's consolidated review and M-n from its re-review (2026-10-18); neither is published, so each corrected statement is given in full. Every corrected value is pre-lab until the named lab runs.

| D-linux-chain-install-15 | C-35 | The tier without a view leaves launchers of unconfined work reachable (the D-Bus session bus, the user manager's private socket, the user-wide node endpoint). Each is a gap of class *escape*, which caps `holds_against` at {A1, A2}; "partly against A1–A4" is withdrawn. `ipc` is NONE below Landlock ABI 6. Ubuntu 24.04 by default gives no boundary against compromised tools or AI-generated commands; it gives resource control plus guardrails. The seccomp socket-family remedy is evaluated in LAB-LNX-01 and LAB-LNX-02 before the tier is promoted. |
| D-linux-chain-install-15 | M-7 | The cap is a property of the realization: `net.out` PARTIAL (TCP ports) and every other dimension of that tier, ENFORCED and MEDIATED included, hold against {A1, A2} only while any escape-class gap remains, and the summary headline states the escape. A launcher cell not measured on the host's build class counts as an escape gap until its lab denies it. |
| D-linux-chain-install-15 | C-36 | `ipc` and `ui_isolation` are computed from the network policy and the Landlock ABI together: under an open network, host abstract sockets (including a display server's) stay reachable unless Landlock abstract-socket scoping (ABI 6 or later) applies. |
