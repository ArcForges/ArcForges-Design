# Acquisition runtimes, extension hosts, updaters and GPU: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 13c (acquisition runtimes, updaters and GPU), including its inline verification markers and 40-row verification log, and the study's acquisition-runtimes digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts read from the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions, including device modes, were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 13c the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 corrected one reference in this file. The table at the end names the affected facts, the ruling and the corrected statement, which governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Reading notes:** Where the report's verification log corrected the original text, the corrected statement and label are used (for example pnpm 10 versus 11 defaults, the `dnx` label of E3, NSSM install at P1). Third-party vendor documentation is E3; E2 is kept for OS vendors. Nothing was executed for this record. "Lab plan" refers to the experiment plans in the report's section G.4; none has run. Privileges are given as ArcForges / workload (P0 = no privilege beyond the user).
