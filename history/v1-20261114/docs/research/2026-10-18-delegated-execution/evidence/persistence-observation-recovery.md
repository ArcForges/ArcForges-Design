# Persistence, privileged-change observation, revocation and recovery: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 13d (persistence, privileged-change observation, recovery and migration), including its inline verification markers, the author's 40-row verification log and the independent verifier's 40-row log, which overrides the author's, and the study's persistence-observation-recovery digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts read from the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host, and no row stands for any particular account's group memberships.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 13d the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated or mis-staged. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Reading notes:** Where the independent verification corrected the original text, the corrected statement and label are used. "VM plan" refers to the disposable-VM experiment plans in the report's section 4.4; none has run. "SPECIFIED" marks a design element that exists in the Design but has not been delivered. Privileges are written P0 (none) to P3 (vendor entitlement plus root). LAB-… IDs name entries of `lab-register.md` in this folder.

| D-persistence-observation-recovery-8 | C-46 | The first stage is the per-attempt persistence diff around managed attempts (`PERSISTENCE_ADDED` with `ATTEMPT(<id>)`, attributable by construction).
