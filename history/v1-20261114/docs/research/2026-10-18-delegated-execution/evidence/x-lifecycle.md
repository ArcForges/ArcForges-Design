# Evidence record: application lifecycle, supply-chain trust, snapshots, policy model and Native AOT (x-lifecycle)

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts. It informs roadmap direction only and is not release-1 verification.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 13b (lifecycle, supply chain, snapshots, policy model and Native AOT), with its inline verification and reconciliation markers and its verification log of 53 checked claims, and its x-lifecycle decision digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet). Corrected text from the verification log and the study reconciliation overrides the original report text.
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). Facts about the study's own workstation and its WSL distributions were removed or restated host-neutrally; no row describes a particular host.
- **Review corrections:** no ruling of the study's review round of 2026-10-18 changed a value in this file; the closing table records one publication note.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges and ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Labels:** E1 ArcForges (proven under the repository gate), E2 OS vendor, E3 mature third party, E4 needs a prototype, E5 speculative, E6 inappropriate. "SPECIFIED" means written in the ArcForges Design but not delivered. "E3-weak" is a third-party claim with weak maturity signals. "✗" means known or expected to emit trim or AOT warnings.
- **Privileges:** "ArcForges / workload", using the study's P0 (standard user) to P3 (entitlement or root) scale.
- **Report section column:** § numbers, D1–D5 and "rank n" (section 12.1 ranked summary) refer to the study report 13b; "verification log #n" is row n of that report's verification log; "study reconciliation Cn" is item n of the study's contradiction reconciliation. The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence. "Study VM plan" means a disposable-VM experiment plan of the study, not yet run; the published lab register gives each its LAB ID. LAB-… IDs name entries of `lab-register.md` in this folder.
