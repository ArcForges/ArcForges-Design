# Evidence record: Linux sandboxing projects, network control, desktop/IPC exposure, portable formats

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-18 delegated-execution study; evidence labels E1–E6 as defined in the study; not verification-register facts.
- **Date:** 2026-10-18.
- **Source reports:** the study report 11b (Linux projects, network and desktop), with its 2026-10-18 verification and reconciliation markers and its verification log, and the study's lnx-projects decision digest (section 1, mechanism fact sheet).
- **Publication:** curated 2026-10-19 for the public ArcForges-Design repository (`docs/research/2026-10-18-delegated-execution/`). No row describes a particular host.
- **Report section column:** names the section of the study report 11b the fact was drawn from ("study reconciliation" is the study's contradiction reconciliation). The study reports, digests and reconciliation are working files of the study and are not published; the primary-source column is the citable evidence.
- **Review corrections:** the study's review round of 2026-10-18 ruled some values in this file overstated. The table at the end names each affected fact, the ruling and the corrected statement. Where a claim and its correction differ, the corrected statement governs. Claim text is left unedited so its provenance stays visible.
- **First-party sources:** repository links point at `main` of ArcForges/ArcForges-Design. "Analysis, no primary source" marks a claim that rests on the study's own reasoning.
- **Use:** input to roadmap direction only. Nothing here is release-1 verification. Privileges are written ArcForges/workload (P0 = none, P1 = one-time root or administrator step). Where a corrected marker in the report changed a claim, the corrected text is used.

The corrected statement governs where it differs from the claim above. Rulings named C-nn are from the study's consolidated review and M-n from its re-review (2026-10-18); neither is published, so each corrected statement is given in full. Every corrected value is pre-lab until the named lab runs.

| D-lnx-projects-38, D-lnx-projects-39 | C-35; M-7 | The tier of Landlock plus seccomp without namespaces leaves the session bus, the user manager's private socket and the user-wide node endpoint reachable unless a policy filter closes them; each reachable launcher is a gap of class *escape*, which caps every dimension of the realization at {A1, A2}. `ipc` is NONE below ABI 6. Ubuntu 24.04 by default gives no boundary against compromised tools or AI-generated commands; it gives resource control plus guardrails. | D-lnx-projects-14, D-lnx-projects-32 | C-37 | The socket-family allowlist of N0 and N1 and the TIOCSTI and TIOCLINUX denial are policy filters: required, read back by filter count and policy digest, and counted toward `net`, `ipc` and `ui`.
