# Cooperative resource coordination semantics: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's cooperative resource-coordination API track (report 16), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** nothing here is built. ArcForges values are at most E4, untested in any lab, and rest on a SPECIFIED or planned scheduler (AF-SCHED-01, AF-RES-01). E4/E5 items are never guarantees.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-resource-api-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n").
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - An **advisory grant** goes to an application that is not contained; an **enforced grant** resizes the requester's own attempt or container. **Same-user qualifier:** enforcement holds against A1 and A2 only, because same-user code can alter ArcForges' containers (Design 11, threat T-22). "4 cores" means 4,000 millicores of CPU time.
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [11](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/11-security-privacy.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)); R-… and AF-… IDs are the Design's requirements and the Plan's tasks.
  - Status words follow the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12); the hold term is written `K` per resource (Design 03 §10 as amended). Corrections are listed in "Review corrections".
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s; `V-W…` and `V-L…` are verification-register entries.
