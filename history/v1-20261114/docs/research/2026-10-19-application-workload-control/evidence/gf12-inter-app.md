# Interactions between applications and delegated operations: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's gap-fill on interactions between applications (report 3a), including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; nothing is a measured ArcForges result. Report 3a read no host fact and no ArcForges code; its ArcForges evidence is Design text. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf12-inter-app-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 3a).
- **Bottom line of the gap-fill:** adopt delegated work (IA-3) and the exposure computation of shared services (IA-4); integrate the hand-off broker (IA-1) only after it beats the in-process baseline, and owner hand-off rules (IA-2) only after the owner's decision on AD-0017 boundary 3.4; disclose inbound hand-offs (IA-7); reject clipboard and drag mediation (IA-5, IA-6). Zero new concepts, conditionally. Nothing needs P1–P3. Release 1 is untouched.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. SPECIFIED is Design text, not delivered; "analysis" is the report's reasoning, never above E4.
  - **A** is the source attempt; **B** the target Installation. Routes: **in-process child** (inherits A's container); **brokered** (a shell, portal, bus or launch service starts the handler outside A's ancestry); **node-mediated** (over A's attempt endpoint, the node starts B as a task).
  - The rule labels P-1 … P-9 and the opportunity labels IA-1 … IA-7 are defined by the facts below. R1 … R7, SR-3, T-28, T-29 and T-42 are [chapter 17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)'s.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
