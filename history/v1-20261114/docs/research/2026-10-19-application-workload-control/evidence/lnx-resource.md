# Linux cgroup v2 and systemd control of applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's Linux owner-directed resource-control track (report 13), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections". "E3 (source)" means read from upstream source code, never measured. Linux adoption is not built in release 1 (`Adopt = Unsupported`, E1), so every application-facing value is E2–E5 until the lab runs.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host: the study's observations on a development machine are cited only by their verification-register IDs (L-…), or restated as general facts.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-lnx-resource-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n").
- **Clauses not published:** the clauses of D2-lnx-resource-9 and -16 that commented on delivered release-1 records are recorded outside this record, for the product owner and the owning tasks' reviewers, and are removed here.
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API. P0 is the same user; P1 an administrator step.
  - Supported targets (AD-0002): Debian 12 (systemd 252) and 13 (257), Ubuntu 22.04 (249) and 24.04 (255); Fedora is research only.
  - Strength: ENFORCED holds against A1 and A2. Every value is a GUARDRAIL against an application that targets ArcForges' or systemd's objects (Design 11, threat T-22), unless a confined launch closes every launcher (D2-lnx-resource-22).
  - Status words follow the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12); words the track wrote before that reconciliation are mapped in place (see "Review corrections").
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts and `LAB-…` entries are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s. `L-…` and `V-L…` IDs are entries of the [verification register](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/verification/platform-capabilities.md).
