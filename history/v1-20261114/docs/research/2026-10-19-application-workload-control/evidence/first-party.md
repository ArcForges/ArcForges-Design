# The first-party reference (ArcSlate as built, unchanged): evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's first-party reference-model track (report 1a), including its adversarial verification log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their verified form; nothing ran, so no new ArcForges capability exceeds E4. The corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **ArcSlate is unchanged.** The study read ArcSlate's code (commit `569785a`) and changed nothing; every first-party adaptation below is design guidance for a later first-party decision.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-first-party-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of the study's digest, "digest §n", and of report 1a where the digest names one).
- **Not published:** D2-first-party-14, a nested display server per application, a GUI-isolation remedy outside the study's scope (the scope correction of 2026-10-19).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - Labels: **E1-DP** is first-party DesktopPlatform code; **code fact** is what first-party source says; **SPECIFIED** is designed, not delivered; **pointer** is a single post or report.
  - Channel terms: a **drive cell** (a launcher cell) lets confined code make unconfined code act; it is an (A) bypass that caps every dimension at {A1, A2} (accidental and over-privileged code) unless shown denied (chapter 17 §1.4; the 2026-10-18 record's ruling M-8). A **read cell** is disclosed. An **inbound** channel is (B) only. W2 is chapter 17's AppContainer tier.
  - Design chapters are cited by number ([03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md), [04](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-execution.md), [11](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/11-security-privacy.md), [12](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/12-client-applications.md), [17](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/17-delegated-execution.md)); R1 … R7, SR-3 and T-28 are chapter 17's.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md). `D-…` facts, `LAB-…` entries and the rulings C-45 and M-8 are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s.
