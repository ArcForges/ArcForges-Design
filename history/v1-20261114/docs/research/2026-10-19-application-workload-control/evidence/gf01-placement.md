# Placement, accounting and default semantics of owner-directed applications: evidence record

- **Scope:** research evidence of the 2026-10-19 vision-correction study (application and workload control); not verification-register facts. Evidence labels are defined in this folder's [README](../README.md).
- **Date:** 2026-10-19.
- **Source:** the study's placement, accounting and default-semantics reconciliation (report 30), which reconciled the tracks' disagreements about where an owner's application sits and what an owner's number means, including its independent challenge log, and the study's digest of it. Claims appear in their challenged form; the corrections made after the digest are listed in "Review corrections".
- **Status:** research only. Everything is roadmap direction at E4 and authorizes nothing. Release 1 is unchanged and has no owner placement.
- **Publication:** curated for the public ArcForges-Design repository. No row describes a particular host.
- **Columns:** **ID** `D2-gf01-placement-<n>` is stable; numbers are not reassigned. **Claim** states the fact; the text after "For ArcForges:" is the study's roadmap-level consequence, never a decision. **Evidence** gives the label(s), then the integration levels, whether the application must cooperate, and the platform. **Report section** is provenance only (the section of report 30).
- **Reading notes:**
  - Integration levels: L1 observe; L2 apply resource controls to a running process; L3 launch and supervise; L4 launch with an OS-enforced access sandbox; L5 voluntary SDK or local API.
  - **Managed placement:** under the root (an attempt job in the root job; a unit in `arcforges.slice`); release 1's only placement. **Owner placement:** beside the root (a top-level job; an XDG-named unit in `app.slice`, or the desktop's own unit).
  - Symbols are [Design 03](https://github.com/ArcForges/ArcForges-Design/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-resource-control.md) §2's. The hold term is written `K` and defined per resource (Design 03 §10 as amended); it is distinct from Design 03 §6's host share `H_cpu`. Status words are the study's one vocabulary (D2-gf02-vocabulary-12). Every value holds against A1 and A2 only.
  - `LAB2-…` IDs name entries of [lab-register.md](../lab-register.md); G-numbered gates are the two registers' gates. `D-…` facts are the [2026-10-18 record](../../2026-10-18-delegated-execution/README.md)'s. T-22 and T-23 are Design 11's threats.
